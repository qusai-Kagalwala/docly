# Docly privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Docly is a Windows app that prepares photos, signatures and PDFs for online
forms. It is made by Qusai Kagalwala.

## What Docly collects

Nothing. Docly does not collect, store, send or share any personal
information.

- Docly works entirely on your computer and makes no network connections:
  no analytics, no crash reporting, no ads, no account and no sign-in.
- The photos, signatures and PDFs you open are processed on your PC only.
  Results are saved as new files where you choose; your originals are never
  changed.
- Docly keeps two small files in its own app-data folder on your PC: your
  settings (such as light or dark mode and your output folder) and a log of
  what the app did, used to diagnose problems. They never leave your PC, and
  uninstalling Docly removes them.
- Links in the app (for example to GitHub or LinkedIn) open in your web
  browser only when you click them. Those websites have their own privacy
  policies.

## Children

Docly collects no information from anyone, including children.
